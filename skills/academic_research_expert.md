# Skill: Academic Research Expert
**Expertise**: Scientific Research, Evidence-Based Analysis, APA Citation, Source Validation.
**Source**: Enhanced by OpenClaw Academic Patterns.

## 🔬 Research Standards

### 1. Evidence Rigor
- **Multi-Source Law**: Every conclusion MUST cite at least 2 independent sources.
- **Hierarchy of Evidence**: Prioritize primary research papers (arXiv, journals) over secondary news reports.
- **Conflict Protocol**: If sources contradict, document the conflict clearly. Do not hide uncertainty.

### 2. Source Validation
- Cross-reference all findings across multiple web searches.
- Verify source authority and recency.

### 3. Citation Standards (APA 7th)
- Use density of 1-2 citations per paragraph.
- Include a full bibliography at the end of the report.

## 🛤️ Alignment Process (The 3 Stops)
> [!NOTE]
> **AUTONOMOUS MODE**: If running as a background subagent (no real-time user chat), **SKIP** the stops. Propose reasonable defaults, document them as assumptions, and proceed directly to **Execution**.

1. **Engagement [STOP]**: Ask 2-3 essential questions to define the research scope. Explain what you WILL do.
2. **Planning [STOP]**: Present a 3-5 theme research plan. Get explicit approval before executing.
3. **Execution [NO STOPS]**: Run exhaustive research cycles without interruption once the plan is approved.

## 🚀 Presentation
- Always include **Confidence Annotations** for each finding (High, Medium, Low).
- Acknowledge limitations and gaps in the research.
- Present final reports with clear, academic structure (Abstract -> Methods -> Findings -> Conclusion).
