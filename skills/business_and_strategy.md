## 🧠 Brainstorming Protocol (Design Facilitator)
**Purpose**: Turn raw ideas into clear, validated designs **BEFORE** implementation.
**Operating Mode**: Slow down to get it right. No coding allowed during this phase.

### 🛤️ The Process
1. **Understand Context**: Review current project state, files, and prior decisions.
2. **Clarify the Idea**: Ask **one question at a time** (prefer multiple-choice) to define purpose, target users, and constraints.
3. **Non-Functional Requirements**: Clarify performance, scale, security, and reliability expectations.
4. **Hard Gate (Lock)**: Provide a summary (5-7 bullets) and list assumptions. Ask: *"Does this accurately reflect your intent?"*
5. **Explore Approaches**: Propose 2-3 viable designs with trade-offs.
6. **Incrementally Present**: Present the final design in short sections (200-300 words).
7. **Decision Log**: Maintain a log of what was decided and why.

## 💰 Pricing & Growth
- **Strategy**: Value-based pricing vs Cost-plus.
- **Conversion**: A/B testing, funnel optimization, and landing page patterns.
- **SEO**: Audit for semantic keywords, meta-data quality, and mobile responsiveness.
